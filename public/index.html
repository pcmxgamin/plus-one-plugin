When +1 runs inside ChatGPT, ChatGPT can use relevant conversation context and memory to personalise the lesson. Your full conversation archive is not downloaded into +1.

How it works

1. You connect +1 in ChatGPT.

2. You choose whether relevant ChatGPT context can be used for personalisation.

3. ChatGPT passes a compact relevant summary to +1—not your raw history.

4. +1 picks today’s useful lesson and records only your learning profile and Capability Passport.
